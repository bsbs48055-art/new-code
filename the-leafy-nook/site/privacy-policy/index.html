Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how The Leafy Nook (“we,” “us,” or “this site”) collects, uses, and shares information when you visit theleafynook.com (the “Site”).

Information we collect

Information you provide directly

If you use our contact form or subscribe to our newsletter, we collect the information you voluntarily provide, such as your name and email address, solely to respond to your message or to send the newsletter content you signed up for.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we (and our third-party service providers) may automatically collect certain information when you visit the Site, including your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, time spent on pages, and referring URLs. This is typically collected through cookies and similar tracking technologies, and through analytics tools such as Google Analytics.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We and our third-party partners (including advertising and analytics providers) use cookies to:

  • Understand how visitors use the Site so we can improve it.
  • Remember preferences between visits.
  • Serve and measure the performance of advertising, including personalized ads.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect certain features of the Site.

Google AdSense and advertising

This Site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense and/or other third-party advertising networks. These networks may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to collect information about your visits to this and other websites in order to provide advertisements about goods and services that may interest you.

  • Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s Ads Settings.
  • You may also opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.
  • Third-party vendors, including Google, may show our ads on sites across the internet.

We do not control the cookies or tracking technologies used by third-party advertising networks, and this Privacy Policy does not apply to, and we are not responsible for, the privacy practices of those third parties. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party ad networks for more detailed information.

Analytics

We may use analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to help us understand how visitors engage with the Site. These services may use cookies and similar technologies to collect and analyze information about Site use. You can learn more about Google Analytics’ data practices and opt out at Google’s opt-out page.

How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Site and its content.
  • Respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form.
  • Send newsletter content to subscribers who have opted in, if applicable.
  • Understand aggregate usage patterns to improve our content and user experience.
  • Support advertising, as described above.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Third-party service providers who help operate the Site (such as hosting providers, analytics services, and advertising networks), who are authorized to use your information only as necessary to provide these services.
  • Law enforcement or other parties when required by law, or to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of others.

Children’s privacy

This Site is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Your choices

  • You can opt out of personalized advertising as described in the “Google AdSense and advertising” section above.
  • You can unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time using the link provided in any email we send.
  • You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact page or email us at hello@theleafynook.com.
