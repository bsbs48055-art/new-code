## Our editorial standards

The Leafy Nook publishes original, independently written content about houseplant care. This page explains how our content is created and maintained so readers know what to expect.

## How we research and write

- Every article is written specifically for this site — we do not publish spun, auto-generated, or scraped content.
- Care recommendations are based on widely accepted horticultural knowledge, cross-referenced across multiple reputable gardening and botanical resources, combined with hands-on experience keeping the plants we write about.
- Where advice depends heavily on individual conditions (home humidity, light levels, water hardness, climate), we say so explicitly rather than presenting a single number as universally correct.
- We link to other articles on this site where a topic is covered in more depth elsewhere, rather than repeating the same explanation across multiple posts.

## Corrections and updates

Houseplant care knowledge evolves, and we sometimes get things wrong or need to update guidance as we learn more. If you spot an error or have a correction to suggest, please reach out via our [Contact page](/contact/) with details, including a link to the article. We review and, where warranted, correct or update published articles.

## Advertising and affiliate disclosure

This site may display advertising through Google AdSense and/or other advertising networks. Ads are served by third parties and are clearly distinguished from editorial content. Should we include affiliate links to products we genuinely recommend in the future, they will be clearly disclosed within the relevant article. Advertising and any potential affiliate relationships never influence the substance of our care recommendations — our advice is the same whether or not a given product is mentioned.

## Author and contact information

Content on this site is produced by The Leafy Nook's editorial team. For questions about a specific article, sourcing, or to request a correction, use our [Contact page](/contact/).
