# Beauty Glow Hub — Keyword Plan (Reach More Readers)

**Truth first:** the `keywords` meta tag (now added to your theme) is **ignored by
Google** for ranking. Big traffic comes from targeting the keywords below inside
your **post titles, first paragraph, headings and content** — and publishing lots
of helpful posts. Use this list as your content roadmap.

## How to use a keyword (do this for every post)
1. Pick **one main keyword** from the list.
2. Put it in the **title**, the **custom permalink**, the **first sentence**, and one **H2**.
3. Sprinkle 3–5 **related keywords** naturally through the post (don't stuff).
4. Write the **Search Description** (150–160 chars) using the main keyword.
5. Add matching **labels** and 2–3 **internal links** to related posts.

> Tip: "long‑tail" keywords (3–5 words) are easier to rank for and convert better
> than single words. Start with those.

## Where to find more keywords (free)
- **Google autocomplete** — start typing your topic and note the suggestions.
- **"People also ask"** and **"Related searches"** on Google results.
- **Google Trends** (trends.google.com) — spot rising beauty topics.
- **Pinterest search bar** — beauty is huge on Pinterest; the suggestions are gold.
- **Google Keyword Planner** / **Ubersuggest** (free tiers) for search volume.

Publish consistently around these, do the Search Console steps in `SEO-GUIDE.md`,
and share every post on Pinterest — that's the realistic path to big traffic.
