Aloe vera has been used for centuries for good reason. This humble plant is a gentle multitasker for both skin and hair. Here are the real aloe vera benefits and how to use it.

Benefits for Skin

  • Soothes sunburn and irritation.
  • Lightweight hydration, great for oily skin.
  • Calms redness after shaving or exfoliating.

Benefits for Hair

Aloe can hydrate the scalp and add slip for easier detangling. Apply pure gel, leave briefly, then rinse.

How to Use Aloe Vera

  1. Choose pure aloe gel without added alcohol or fragrance.
  2. Patch-test before first use.
  3. Use as a light moisturiser, soothing mask, or scalp treatment.
Look for the shortest ingredient list — the closer to pure aloe, the better.

Conclusion

Aloe vera is a cheap, gentle addition to your natural routine. Explore more natural remedies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I leave aloe vera on my face overnight?

Yes, pure aloe gel can be left on as a light overnight treatment for most skin types. Patch-test first.

Is aloe vera good for oily skin?

Yes — it hydrates without heaviness, making it a great lightweight option for oily and combination skin.
