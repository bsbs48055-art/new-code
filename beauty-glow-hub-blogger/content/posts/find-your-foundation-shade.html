Nothing ruins a look faster than the wrong foundation shade. The trick is understanding two things: your undertone and your depth. Here is how to find your perfect foundation shade every time.

Step 1: Find Your Undertone

  • Cool: veins look blue; silver jewellery flatters.
  • Warm: veins look green; gold jewellery flatters.
  • Neutral: a mix of both.

Step 2: Find Your Depth

Match foundation to your jaw or neck, not your hand — your hand is often a different tone. The right shade should almost disappear.

Step 3: Buying Online

Use brand shade-finder tools and cross-check against a foundation you already love. Read reviews from people with your skin tone.

Undertone first, depth second. Get those right and your base will look seamless.

Conclusion

Test in daylight and give it a few minutes to settle before deciding. For more, see our makeup guides.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know my foundation undertone?

Check your veins (blue = cool, green = warm), and which jewellery suits you (silver = cool, gold = warm).

Where should I test foundation?

On your jaw or neck in natural daylight — never on your hand, which is usually a different tone.
