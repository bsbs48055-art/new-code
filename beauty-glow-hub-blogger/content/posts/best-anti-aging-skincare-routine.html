The best time to start an anti-ageing routine is now — but the right steps change as you get older. Here is a simple anti-aging skincare routine by age, built around ingredients that actually have science behind them.

In Your 20s: Prevention

  • Daily sunscreen (the #1 anti-ageing step).
  • A gentle cleanser and moisturiser.
  • Vitamin C in the morning for antioxidant protection.

In Your 30s: Protect + Repair

  • Everything above, plus a gentle retinol at night.
  • Add an eye cream if you notice fine lines.
  • Stay consistent with SPF.

In Your 40s and Beyond: Nourish

  • Richer moisturisers with ceramides and peptides.
  • Continue retinol (as tolerated) and vitamin C.
  • Focus on hydration and barrier support.

The Ingredients That Matter Most

Sunscreen, retinoids, vitamin C, niacinamide and peptides are the proven players. You do not need dozens of products — just the right few, used consistently.

Sunscreen every day does more for ageing skin than any expensive cream.

Conclusion

Match your routine to your stage of life and stay consistent. New to actives? Start with our retinol for beginners guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I start an anti-aging routine?

Prevention in your 20s is ideal — mainly daily sunscreen and antioxidants. But it is never too late to start protecting and repairing your skin.

What is the best anti-aging ingredient?

Sunscreen for prevention and retinoids for repair are the two most proven. Vitamin C, niacinamide and peptides are strong supporting players.
