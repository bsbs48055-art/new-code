There is nothing worse than makeup that slides off by lunchtime. With a few pro habits, you can make your makeup last all day. Here is exactly how.

Prep Is Everything

Start with moisturised, balanced skin and a primer suited to your skin type — mattifying for oily, hydrating for dry.

Build in Thin Layers

Thin, well-blended layers last far longer than one thick coat that creases and slides.

Set It Strategically

  • Lightly powder only where you get oily.
  • Finish with a setting spray to meld everything together.
  • Carry blotting papers for touch-ups instead of piling on more powder.
Great longevity is 80% skin prep and 20% product.

Conclusion

Prep, thin layers and smart setting are the secret. For a quick base, try our everyday makeup routine.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I stop my makeup sliding off?

Prep with the right primer, apply thin layers, set oily areas with powder and finish with a setting spray.

Does setting spray really work?

Yes — a good setting spray melds layers together and noticeably extends wear, especially in heat.
