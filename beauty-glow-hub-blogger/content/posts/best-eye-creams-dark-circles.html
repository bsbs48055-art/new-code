Match the Ingredient to Your Concern

  • Dark circles: vitamin C and caffeine to brighten and de-puff.
  • Fine lines: peptides and gentle retinol.
  • Puffiness: caffeine and cool application.
  • Dryness: hyaluronic acid and ceramides.

How to Apply Eye Cream

Use a rice-grain amount and tap gently with your ring finger — never rub or drag the delicate skin.

Manage expectations: eye creams help, but sleep, hydration and SPF matter just as much.

Conclusion

Frequently Asked Questions

Do eye creams really work for dark circles?

They help, especially caffeine and vitamin C formulas, but results depend on the cause. Sleep and hydration make a big difference too.

When should I apply eye cream?

Morning and night, before your moisturiser, tapping gently with your ring finger.
