Great hair starts at the roots. A healthy scalp creates the ideal environment for strong, shiny growth, yet it is the step most people skip. Here is a simple scalp care routine that makes a real difference.

Why Your Scalp Matters

The scalp is skin — and like facial skin it can get dry, oily, flaky or congested. Buildup and imbalance can weaken the hair follicle over time.

A Simple Scalp Routine

  • Cleanse regularly to remove oil and product buildup.
  • Exfoliate gently once a week if you are prone to flaking.
  • Massage for a few minutes to boost circulation.
  • Protect from sunburn and over-styling.

Habits That Help

Avoid very hot water, do not overload the roots with heavy products, and eat a balanced diet for healthy growth.

Treat your scalp with the same care as your face and your hair will reward you.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I keep my scalp healthy?

Cleanse regularly, exfoliate gently once a week, massage to boost circulation, and avoid heavy buildup and very hot water.

Does scalp massage help hair growth?

Regular gentle massage can improve circulation and is a low-risk, relaxing habit that may support healthier growth.
